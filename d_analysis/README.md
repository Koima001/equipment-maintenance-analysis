This section presents key findings from the equipment maintenance analysis, highlighting operational performance, maintenance costs, and actionable recommendations for improving efficiency and cost management.

## Key Findings

1. **Maintenance cost distribution:** Parts expenditure accounts for approximately KES 106.86 million, considerably exceeding labour costs of KES 23.27 million. This indicates that spare parts are the primary contributor to maintenance expenditure.

2. **Site-level expenditure:** SITE-04 records the highest maintenance expenditure, followed by SITE-01, SITE-02 and SITE-03. This suggests differences in equipment maintenance demands or operating conditions across sites.

3. **Maintenance work types:** Preventive and corrective maintenance account for the largest expenditure, while inspection and emergency maintenance contribute relatively less.

4. **Equipment and priority costs:** Maintenance expenditure varies across equipment types and priority levels, highlighting differences in maintenance requirements and resource allocation.

5. **Manufacturer expenditure:** Parts expenditure differs considerably across manufacturers and equipment categories. Summit Mechanical records particularly high expenditure on pumps, while Atlas Industrial records substantial expenditure on compressors.

6. **Monthly expenditure trends:** Maintenance costs fluctuate throughout the year. January records the highest expenditure, while June records the lowest. Expenditure increases again towards November.

## Recommendations

1. **Optimize spare parts procurement:** Review frequently purchased parts, negotiate supplier contracts and explore cost-effective sourcing strategies to reduce parts expenditure.

2. **Investigate high-cost sites:** Conduct further assessments at SITE-04 and SITE-01 to identify equipment, maintenance practices or operating conditions contributing to higher costs.

3. **Strengthen preventive maintenance:** Review preventive maintenance schedules to identify opportunities to reduce costly corrective interventions and unexpected equipment failures.

4. **Evaluate supplier performance:** Compare manufacturer costs, parts quality and equipment reliability to support informed procurement and supplier selection.

5. **Monitor monthly expenditure:** Investigate periods of unusually high expenditure and establish monthly maintenance cost monitoring to improve budget planning.

## Conclusion

The analysis identifies spare parts as the largest contributor to maintenance expenditure, with notable differences across sites, equipment categories and manufacturers. Improving procurement practices, preventive maintenance and cost monitoring could help reduce maintenance expenditure and improve operational efficiency.
